# 🎨 Choose Your Vibe

## Make It Personal

One of the coolest things about vibe coding? You get to build something that actually matters to **you**. Not a boring "todo list" tutorial—a real application in an industry you care about.

## Pick Your Industry

Choose the vertical that excites you most. Don't overthink it—you can always come back and try different themes!

### 🏪 Retail & E-Commerce

**Build:** A modern product catalog and shopping experience

**You'll Create:**

- Product showcase with categories
- Interactive shopping cart
- Real-time inventory displays
- Order management dashboard

**Perfect For:** People interested in online business, product management, or retail technology

**Theme Colors:** Professional blue with modern gradients

**Sample Data:** Products, orders, customer analytics, inventory levels

## What Makes Each Theme Different?

Every theme gives you:

✅ **Custom color palette** - Your app will look professionally themed  
✅ **Industry-specific data** - Realistic mock data that makes sense  
✅ **Tailored prompts** - AI instructions optimized for your industry  
✅ **Relevant features** - Functionality that matches real-world needs

But here's the secret: **The underlying tech is the same!**

You're learning React, APIs, and modern web development—the industry theme just makes it more fun and relevant to you.

## 🏆 Pro Tip: Theme Master Achievement

Try all six themes to unlock the **🎨 Theme Master** badge! Each theme teaches you slightly different patterns and approaches, making you a more well-rounded developer.

## Can't Decide?

### Pick Based on Your Goal:

**Want to build a business?** → Retail or Finance  
**Work in healthcare?** → Healthcare  
**Into logistics/operations?** → Transportation or Manufacturing  
**Engineering background?** → Oil & Gas or Manufacturing  
**Just want something cool?** → Pick whatever looks fun!

### Still Stuck?

**Close your eyes and point at the screen.** Seriously! They're all great learning experiences. The best choice is the one that gets you started.

---

## What Happens After You Choose?

1. **Theme Activates**: Your portal's colors and styling adapt
2. **Data Loads**: Industry-specific mock data appears
3. **Prompts Customize**: AI instructions tailor to your vertical
4. **Labs Personalize**: Examples match your chosen industry

Everything from this point forward will be themed to your selection!

## Ready to Choose?

Click your industry in the app to begin! Your adventure in vibe coding is about to get personalized.

Remember: **There's no wrong choice.** Pick what excites you and let's start building!

  - https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-apps/developer/code-apps/troubleshoot-add-datasource
  - Use this if you’re building/deploying a Power Apps Code Apps experience and a data source/connector won’t add or authenticate.

- **Microsoft Edge: Control a website’s access to the local network**
  - https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/topic/control-a-website-s-access-to-the-local-network-in-microsoft-edge-ef7eff4c-676d-4105-935c-2acbcd841d51
  - Use this if your app (or Power Apps Code Apps) can’t reach a localhost or on-network resource due to browser security restrictions.
